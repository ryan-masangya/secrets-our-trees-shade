04 / Ethics

What the trees can (and can't) tell us

The census was conducted by NYC Parks and partner organizations from May 2015 to October 2016. Tree health reflects the surveyor's perception at the time. Stewardship records signs of care; it does not prove that care caused a tree to be healthier.

Source: NYC Open Data — 2015 Street Tree Census . This prototype loads a changeable sample of 5,000–20,000 records from the public dataset. It is a sample, not a complete census analysis.
